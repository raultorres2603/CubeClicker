# Spec Review — r1

## Summary

❌ **Changes requested — 15 blocking findings (2 BLOCKER, 13 MAJOR), 5 non-blocking.** The spec is unusually thorough on the grant carrier, pose arithmetic, bundle/deferred-chunk boundaries, task ordering and Q-1…Q-18 ownership, and those parts verify cleanly against the source. The blocking themes are: (1) a pinned CSS technique that cannot work — `filter: drop-shadow()` and `clip-path` on the same element clips the outer shadow, so AC-18's depth/glow is unachievable as written; (2) an unachievable performance metric — AC-15's average ≤ 16.7 ms `requestAnimationFrame` trace contradicts the pinned 30 fps reveal cap; (3) an incomplete supersession ledger that leaves `theme.ts`, `game/session.ts`/`game/**` and `reference-image-game-view` AC-8 protected while the tasks edit/style them; (4) live-region and modality/focus conflicts with accepted single-live-region and non-modal contracts; and (5) several AC/task ownership and testability gaps (the 8-ray epic flourish, the `RevealPhase` `'idle'` first render, the desktop spotlight's over-constrained base CSS, the unpinned `Crew` heading, minimized HUD and pre-welcome stats). The spec/plan/tasks must be revised before building.

## Verdict

**`changes_requested`** — the two BLOCKERs (F1, F2) and thirteen MAJORs (F3–F15) are open. The spec may not proceed to building until the CSS shadow technique, the AC-15 metric, the supersession ledger, the live-region/modality decisions, the AC/task ownership gaps and the testability gaps are corrected in `spec.md`, `plan.md` and `tasks.md`.
